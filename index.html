How Mathly works 🧭

  1. Pick a chapter 📚Each chapter is one part of the Class 3 syllabus, from place value to fractions.
  2. Learn it 📖A short lesson with a picture, the method step by step, words to know and what to watch out for.
  3. Try it ✏️Two practice questions at the end of every lesson, with no score and no clock.
  4. Quiz yourself ⏱️Mix any kinds of question, choose Easy, Just right or Challenge, and collect stars.

Everything is saved on this device only — no login, no adverts, nothing sent anywhere. Grown-ups can open the question self-test to see every question type being checked.
