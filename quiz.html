What the levels mean 🎚️

  • 🌱 Easy — small numbers, mostly 2 digits, two numbers per sum.
  • 🌼 Just right — the Class 3 level: 3-digit numbers and tables to 10.
  • 🚀 Challenge — bigger numbers, longer sums and 4-digit place value.

How marking works ✅

  • Green means correct — one point each.
  • Pink means try again; the right answer is shown underneath.
  • Yellow means you left it empty. Nothing is ever taken away for a wrong answer.
